# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want a community where people can report bugs, disagree about a design, and ask for help without being insulted or harassed. We pledge to make participation in incident-mcp harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience.
- Accepting criticism gracefully and focusing it on the code.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

This project also handles production credentials. Posting someone else's tokens, kubeconfigs, or customer data is not acceptable, including in issues and pull requests.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and in any other space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through a private GitHub advisory, titled so it is clear this is a conduct report:

https://github.com/gaurangomkar/incident-mcp/security/advisories/new

The maintainers will review the report and respond. They may remove comments, commits, or contributions that violate this code, and may ban a contributor for behavior they consider harmful.

Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may be removed from the project.
